Can hidden text control your AI?

Some web pages hide a line you cannot see. An AI that reads the page can see it, and may obey it. This test shows you if your AI does. Nothing here is harmful.

  1. Open the test page. Open the test page
  2. Press Ctrl + A and copy everything on the page.
  3. Paste it into your AI and write: Summarize this page.
  4. Read the last word of the answer.

How to stay safe

  • Never let an AI act on a page without asking you first.
  • Keep secrets, like keys and passwords, out of anything the AI can read.
  • Treat every page the AI reads as untrusted.

This is called indirect prompt injection. It is number one on the OWASP Top 10 for LLM apps.
